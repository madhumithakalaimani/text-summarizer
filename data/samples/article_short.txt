City Library Extends Weekend Hours After Record Visitor Numbers

The Riverside City Library will open two hours earlier on Saturdays and Sundays starting next month. Officials said visitor numbers rose by 30 percent over the past year, with many families arriving as soon as the doors opened. The library board approved the change on Tuesday after a survey of more than 2,000 residents. Most respondents asked for longer weekend hours and more quiet study space. The extra hours will be staffed by part-time employees and student volunteers. The library also plans to add 40 new study desks by the end of the year. Board members said they will review the schedule again in six months.
